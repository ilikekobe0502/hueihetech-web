7 Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

7 Minutes (the "Service") is provided by Hueihe Tech Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, how long we keep it, and how you can manage it.

If you have any questions, email support@hueihetech.com.

Data we collect

DataWhat it isWhat we use it for
Sign-in identifierAn account identifier supplied by Apple or Google when you sign inTo recognize your account. We do not store your Apple or Google name, email address or password
ProfileNickname, date of birth, gender, short introYour nickname and intro are shown to the person you are matched with; date of birth confirms you are 18 or older; gender is used for match filters
PhotosThe profile photos you uploadShown clearly to the other person only after a mutual match or after you both agree to unlock early. Until then they only see a blurred version
Verification dataThe selfie and liveness check captured during verificationOnly to confirm you are the person in your photos. The image is deleted within 24 hours of the check; we keep only the result (passed or not passed)
LocationYour approximate location (to about 100 meters) at the moment you tap SearchOnly to calculate the distance between you and other people for the distance filter
MessagesMessages you send during timed chats and after matchingTo deliver messages, handle reports and keep the Service safe
Match and choice recordsWhen you were matched, the choice you made when time ran out, early-unlock requestsTo determine match results and count daily matches
Reports and blocksThe reason and details of reports you file, and who you have blockedTo review reports and avoid matching you with the same person again
Purchase recordsThe status of subscriptions and add-on purchasesTo provide paid features. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details

What other users can see

  • During a timed chat: your nickname, short intro and blurred photos.
  • After a mutual match, or after you both agree to unlock early: your clear photos.
  • Never shown: your date of birth, gender, location, distance, sign-in account, or the choice you made when time ran out.

If it is not a match, both people only see "No match this time". Nobody is told who chose what.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not use your location or messages for advertising.
  • We do not read your location unless you tap Search.

How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: messages in a matched conversation are kept until either person deletes their account. Timed chats that did not result in a match can no longer be viewed by either person; we keep them for 30 days to handle reports, then delete them.
  • Location: only your most recently reported location is kept. It is overwritten each time you tap Search.
  • Report records: kept for 2 years to prevent people who broke the rules from signing up again.

Service providers

We use the following providers to store or process data on our behalf. They may only process it on our instructions:

  • Render (application servers)
  • Neon (database)
  • Apple and Google (sign-in and payments)
  • A cloud storage service (photos)
  • An identity verification service (comparing your selfie with your photos)

We will update this page when the providers we use change.

Their servers may be located outside the country or region where you live.

Your choices and rights

At any time you can:

  • Edit your nickname, intro, gender and photos on your profile page in the app.
  • Turn off location permission in your device settings. Matching does not work without it.
  • Delete your account and all data. See how to delete your account.
  • Email support@hueihetech.com to ask for access to, or correction of, the data we hold about you.

Notice under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act

In accordance with Article 8 of the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan):

  • Purposes: providing matching and chat services, member management, account security and abuse prevention, handling reports and appeals, and meeting legal obligations.
  • Categories of data: as listed under "Data we collect".
  • Period of use: as listed under "How long we keep data".
  • Region of use: wherever our servers and our providers' servers are located, which may include countries outside Taiwan.
  • Recipients: us, and the providers that process data on our behalf.
  • Method of use: by automated or non-automated means, within the purposes above.
  • Your rights: you may ask to inquire about or review your data, receive a copy, supplement or correct it, have us stop collecting, processing or using it, and have it deleted.
  • If you do not provide data: nickname, date of birth, gender, photos, verification and location are required for matching. Without them you cannot be matched.

Face images and location

Verification requires processing an image of your face, and matching requires your location. Both happen only after you clearly agree:

  • Before you take the verification selfie, the app explains the purpose and asks for your consent.
  • Before reading your location, your device shows its location permission prompt.

You can withdraw consent at any time. If you do, you will not be able to pass verification or be matched. We do not use face images to build identifiers for any other purpose, and we do not share them with other users or advertisers.

  • Legal obligations: keeping transaction records as required by law and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

Age limit

The Service is only for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has signed up, we will disable the account and delete the data.
