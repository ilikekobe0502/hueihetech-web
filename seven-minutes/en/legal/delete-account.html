Delete your 7 Minutes account and data

This page explains how to delete your 7 Minutes account (developer: Hueihe Tech Co., Ltd.) and the data associated with it.

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

Open 7 Minutes → Profile at the top right of the home screen → "Delete account" at the bottom → confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can no longer sign in, email support@hueihetech.com with the subject "Delete 7 Minutes account" and tell us whether you signed in with Apple or Google. We will process the request and reply within 7 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifier
  • Nickname, date of birth, gender and short intro
  • The photos you uploaded and your verification data
  • Your most recently reported location
  • Your chat list and the messages you sent
  • Match and choice records, and filter settings

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. The people you matched with will see that the conversation has ended.

What is kept

  • Records of confirmed violations reported against you: kept for 2 years to prevent people who broke the rules from signing up again.
  • Reports you filed against others: kept in a form that does not identify you, so the review can be completed.
  • Purchase records: kept for the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Data in server backups is cleared within the regular backup cycle (up to 30 days).

Cancel your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store's subscription settings first so you are not charged again.
