Financial Adviser CRM in Singapore: Renewals and Reviews

Updated: 2026-11-02 · Hueihe Tech

For a financial adviser or insurance agent in Singapore, good client management comes down to three things: one profile per client with their policies, family situation and goals; dated reminders for every renewal, birthday and annual review; and a habit of writing a short note after each meeting. Keep WhatsApp for the conversation, keep the record in a proper client book, collect only the personal data you need with the client's consent under the PDPA, and always follow your firm's rules and MAS requirements.

Why advisers lose touch with clients

Most advisers do not lose clients because of a bad recommendation. They lose them because nobody called. A policy renews quietly, a birthday passes without a message, the annual review slips to next year, and a referral that was offered over kopi is never followed up. With a book of a few hundred policyholders, memory and a WhatsApp chat list are not enough.

The fix is not more effort. It is a simple system: every client has one record, every important date has a reminder, and every meeting ends with a short note and a next step.

What to keep in each client profile

  • Contact and channel. Mobile number used on WhatsApp, preferred time to be contacted, preferred language.
  • Policies held. Type of cover, start and renewal dates, premium frequency. Note which policies you look after and which are with other providers, if the client chose to share that.
  • Life stage and goals. Newly married, first child, buying a flat, planning retirement. These are the moments when needs change.
  • Key dates. Birthday, wedding anniversary, children's birthdays if the client is happy to share them.
  • Meeting notes. What you discussed, what the client asked to think about, and any concerns they raised.
  • Referrals. Who referred them, and anyone they offered to introduce you to.
  • Next step. One action with a date, such as "Send comparison of two hospitalisation plans by Friday".

Keep notes factual and limited to what helps you serve the client. Medical and financial details are sensitive. Record them only where your firm's process requires it, and store them where your firm allows.

Using WhatsApp the right way

In Singapore most clients would rather message than take a call, so WhatsApp is where the relationship happens. Treat it as the channel, not the record. After a chat, move the facts into the client profile and set the next reminder. When you reach out, start from the profile so your message refers to something specific: their daughter starting Primary 1, the renovation they mentioned, or the review you promised.

Avoid broadcasting the same promotional text to your whole contact list. It reads as spam, and marketing messages raise their own consent questions.

PDPA and your firm's rules, in plain terms

This is general information, not legal advice. Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) expects organisations to get consent before collecting, using or disclosing personal data, to use it only for purposes the person was told about, to keep it accurate and protected, and to let people withdraw consent. There are also Do Not Call rules for marketing messages to Singapore numbers. As an adviser you work under your firm, so your firm's data and compliance policies, and the requirements set by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), come first. Check with your compliance team on what you may record in a personal app and how client data must be handled.

Practical habits that help: collect only what you need, explain why you are asking, do not keep sensitive details "just in case", lock your phone, and remove records you no longer need.

FAQ

What is the best way for a financial adviser to keep track of policy renewals?
Record each policy with its renewal date in the client profile and set a reminder a few weeks before. Reviewing a reminder list each morning works better than relying on memory or a chat history.
Does Veloce have an insurance template?
No. Veloce has templates for jewellery, watches, luxury bags, fashion, cars, real estate, B2B and a general one. For insurance, you can customise reminders and tags to track renewals, annual reviews and referrals.
Can I use a personal client book app as a financial adviser in Singapore?
That depends on your firm. Advisers must follow their firm’s data and compliance policies and MAS requirements, as well as the PDPA. Check with your compliance team before storing client information in any app.
What does the PDPA mean for keeping client notes?
In general terms, it means getting consent for the personal data you collect, using it only for the purposes you explained, keeping it accurate and secure, and respecting withdrawal of consent. This is not legal advice; consult your firm or the PDPC’s guidance for specifics.
How can I get more referrals from existing clients?
Stay in regular, genuine contact: remember birthdays, run annual reviews on time and follow up promptly. Note who offered an introduction and set a reminder so the referral is not forgotten, and always ask before contacting the person referred.
