Client Follow-Up Reminders: How Often to Contact Clients

Updated: 2026-11-02 · Hueihe Tech

Do not rely on memory. Set reminders for five moments: birthdays and anniversaries, check-ins after a visit or purchase, service or renewal dates, quote follow-ups before the quote expires, and clients you have not contacted for too long. Then match frequency to the client: for example every few weeks for your top clients, monthly for regulars and quarterly for everyone else. When a reminder comes up, send a short message that mentions something specific to that person.

Why follow-ups slip

Front-line sales days are broken into small pieces: walk-ins, deliveries, events, stock checks. "I'll message her next week" gets pushed aside by whatever is in front of you. It is rarely a lack of care. It is the lack of a system that tells you who to contact, and when.

Five reminders every sales associate should set

  1. Birthdays and anniversaries. They repeat every year and are natural reasons to get in touch. They are also often when people buy gifts.
  2. Check-ins after a visit or purchase. A thank-you on the same day, and a short "how is it going?" a week or two later.
  3. Service, maintenance or renewal dates. Watch servicing, jewelry cleaning, a car service, a lease ending. Useful reminders for the client and a reason to see them again.
  4. Quote follow-ups. Check in before the quote's validity date. Offer to answer questions, not to push.
  5. Dormant clients. If a regular has not heard from you in, say, 60 days, reach out before the relationship cools.

These are common starting points, not rules. Adjust them to your trade and to each client. Some clients welcome frequent updates, others prefer a message only when something truly fits.

How to write follow-ups that do not sound generic

  • Mention one specific detail. The piece they tried, the trip they mentioned, the occasion coming up.
  • Keep it short. Write like a normal chat, not a newsletter. Skip "Dear valued customer".
  • End with an easy question. "Would Saturday afternoon work to see it?" is easier to answer than "Let me know".
  • Match the tone to the person. Formal for some clients, warm and casual for others.

FAQ

How often should a sales associate contact clients?
A common approach is by tier: every 2 to 4 weeks for top clients, monthly for regulars, quarterly for others, and within a week of a first visit for new prospects. Adjust to your trade and to what each client prefers.
How do I remember client birthdays?
Save the date in your client app as a yearly reminder, and ideally get notified a few days early so you have time to prepare a message or gift. In Veloce, birthdays and anniversaries repeat every year automatically.
When should I follow up on a quote?
A few days after sending it to check for questions, and again before the quote expires. Keep the tone helpful rather than pushy.
What should I do about clients who have gone quiet?
Set a "no contact for N days" reminder so they come up before the relationship cools, then reach out with something relevant to them, such as a new piece that matches their taste.
