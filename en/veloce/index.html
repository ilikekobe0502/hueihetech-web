Built for the sales floor, not the back office

Everything you need to keep regular clients coming back, on your phone.

1

Voice Notes

Talk for about 30 seconds after a visit. AI turns it into preferences, wishlist items, budget and follow-up reminders. You review everything before saving.

2

Smart reminders

Birthdays and anniversaries every year, check-ins, service due dates, quote follow-ups before they expire, and clients you have not contacted in a while. All in a calendar view.

3

Find Buyers

Pick a category or snap a photo of a product. See which clients are looking for it, who is likely to buy again and whose taste matches, with the reasons.

4

Import old records

Bring in client details from Excel, CSV, PDF orders, notes or a photo of a handwritten page. AI sorts them into purchases, preferences, wishlist items and reminders, skips duplicates, and you confirm before anything is saved.

5

One page per client

Preferences, sizes, wishlist, purchases, quotes, notes and key dates in one place. Track monthly targets and sales by category too.

6

Your data stays yours

Encrypted storage and biometric lock. Your account is personal, so your client book stays with you if you change employer. Templates for jewelry, watches, luxury bags, fashion, cars, real estate and B2B.
