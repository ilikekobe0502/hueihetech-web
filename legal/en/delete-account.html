Delete your Veloce account and data

This page explains how to delete your Veloce account (developer: Hueihe Tech) and its data.

Option 1: Delete in the App (recommended)

Open Veloce → the “Settings” tab → tap your profile card to open “Account settings” → “Delete account” at the bottom → enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can’t sign in or have removed the App, email support@hueihetech.com from your registered address with the subject “Delete account”. We will delete your account and confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Account data (name, email, password, brand settings, industry and category settings)
  • All client records you created: client profiles, preferences, wishlists, notes and voice recordings, reminders, quotes, sales and performance data
  • Device push tokens and AI usage records

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Apart from records we are legally required to keep, we retain no copies. Remaining data in server backups expires within the routine backup cycle (at most 30 days).
